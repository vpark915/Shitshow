Placeholder food models, cleaned up for the game (rescaled to real-world size, origin at the bottom centre, parts and
materials renamed, made non-metallic, heavy scans reduced to about 5,000 triangles or fewer).
They stand in until our own models in Park_Food_Model_List.docx are made.

Every model below is CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): credit the author in the game's credits.
All are from Sketchfab, downloaded through the Objaverse dataset (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse).
